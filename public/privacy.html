Scout Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 30, 2026

Scout ("Scout," "we," "us," or "our") provides tools for discovering, evaluating, organizing, and tracking employment opportunities.

This Privacy Policy explains how Scout collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use Scout.

By using Scout, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The information Scout collects depends on how you use the Service.

Account Information

When you create or use a Scout account, we may collect information such as:

  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • Display name, if provided
  • Account verification status
  • Account creation information
  • Authentication and session information

Authentication may be provided through third-party infrastructure.

Passwords are handled through our authentication provider. Scout does not need access to your plain-text password.

Profile and Preference Information

Scout may allow you to provide information about yourself, your interests, your preferences, and the types of opportunities you are looking for.

This information may be used to personalize Scout, filter opportunities, evaluate potential matches, and provide eligibility-related features.

You control much of this information through your Scout account and may be able to update it as your preferences change.

Activity Within Scout

When you use Scout, we may collect information about how you interact with the Service, including:

  • Jobs you save
  • Jobs you view or interact with
  • Application tracking information
  • Search and filtering settings
  • Matching results
  • Alert or notification preferences
  • Account settings
  • Other actions taken within Scout

We use this information to provide and improve the features you use.

Technical and Security Information

When you access Scout, Scout or its service providers may automatically receive technical information such as:

  • Browser and device information
  • Request information
  • Session identifiers
  • Cookie information
  • Timestamps
  • Referring pages
  • Error information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Security-related information

This information may be used to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Scout.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through Scout to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Maintain secure sessions
  • Personalize job discovery
  • Match opportunities to user preferences
  • Provide eligibility-related features
  • Provide search and filtering functionality
  • Save jobs
  • Track applications at your request
  • Provide alerts and notifications
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Protect Scout and its users
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Maintain and improve Scout
  • Develop new features
  • Understand how the Service is used
  • Administer subscriptions where applicable
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Scout does not make hiring decisions on behalf of employers.

3. Automated Matching and Analysis

Scout may automatically analyze job information and compare it with information or preferences associated with your account.

Scout may use this analysis to provide:

  • Matching results
  • Rankings
  • Filters
  • Eligibility indicators
  • Recommendations
  • Warnings
  • Explanations

These features are intended to help users evaluate opportunities more efficiently.

They are informational only.

Scout does not guarantee that a match, recommendation, eligibility indicator, or other automated result is accurate or that you will qualify for or receive any opportunity.

You should review the employer's original posting before making decisions or submitting an application.

4. Job Information and External Sources

Scout collects and organizes publicly available employment information from external sources.

This may include:

  • Employer information
  • Job titles
  • Job descriptions
  • Qualifications
  • Compensation information where available
  • Application requirements
  • Application links
  • Posting dates
  • Source information
  • Other information associated with employment opportunities

This information generally concerns job opportunities rather than Scout users.

Scout may direct you to websites operated by employers, applicant tracking systems, job boards, or other third parties.

Once you leave Scout, information you provide to those third parties is governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

Scout does not control how those third parties collect, store, or use information.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Scout may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes including:

  • Keeping users signed in
  • Maintaining authentication sessions
  • Protecting accounts
  • Preventing unauthorized requests
  • Remembering settings
  • Maintaining application functionality
  • Measuring technical performance
  • Preventing abuse

Some cookies may be necessary for Scout to function properly.

If Scout uses optional analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking technologies, additional disclosures or consent controls may be provided where required by law.

6. Payments

If Scout offers paid services, payments may be processed through a third-party payment provider.

The payment provider may collect information needed to complete and administer a transaction.

Scout may receive limited information concerning:

  • Customer accounts
  • Subscription status
  • Subscription plans
  • Transaction status
  • Payment status
  • Renewal status
  • Cancellation status

Scout does not need to store complete payment card information.

Payment providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Analytics and Error Monitoring

Scout may use analytics, performance monitoring, or error-reporting services to understand how Scout is used and identify technical problems.

Depending on the services enabled, these providers may receive information relating to:

  • Pages or features used
  • Browser or device characteristics
  • Performance information
  • Error information
  • Interaction events
  • Technical request information

Scout does not intentionally send passwords or complete payment card information to analytics services.

Where required, optional analytics technologies will be subject to appropriate consent controls.

8. How We Share Information

Scout may disclose information to service providers that help us operate the Service.

These providers may support functions such as:

  • Authentication
  • Database hosting
  • Cloud infrastructure
  • Communications
  • Payment processing
  • Analytics
  • Error monitoring
  • Security
  • Other technical services

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and subject to applicable contractual and legal obligations.

Scout may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law
  • Respond to valid legal process
  • Protect Scout or its users
  • Investigate fraud or abuse
  • Address security incidents
  • Enforce agreements
  • Protect legal rights
  • Respond to emergencies involving safety

If Scout is involved in a merger, financing, acquisition, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar transaction, information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to applicable law.

9. Sale of Personal Information

Scout does not sell personal information for money.

Scout does not rent user profiles or account information to employers or data brokers.

If Scout's practices change in a way that legally constitutes a sale or sharing of personal information under applicable law, this Privacy Policy will be updated and any required rights or controls will be provided.

10. Employers

Creating a Scout account does not automatically make your Scout profile available to employers.

Scout does not provide employers with private account information merely because Scout displays an employer's job posting.

If Scout introduces a feature that allows users to intentionally share profile or application information with employers, Scout will explain how that sharing works before the information is submitted.

11. Data Retention

Scout retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the Service
  • Maintain user accounts
  • Maintain security
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Resolve disputes
  • Meet legal obligations

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods.

When an account is deleted, Scout will delete or de-identify associated information within a reasonable period, except where retention is reasonably necessary for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Security
  • Fraud prevention
  • Dispute resolution
  • Backups
  • Other legitimate purposes permitted by law

Residual copies may remain temporarily in backups or logs until those systems are rotated or deleted.

12. Security

Scout uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information.

These may include measures such as:

  • Secure authentication
  • Access controls
  • Server-side authorization
  • Encrypted network connections
  • Database access restrictions
  • Session security
  • Rate limiting
  • Security monitoring
  • Separation of privileged functionality

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and maintaining control over access to their accounts.

If you believe your Scout account has been compromised, contact Scout through its official support channels.

13. Processing by Service Providers

Scout and its service providers may process information using infrastructure located in different jurisdictions.

Information handled by service providers may therefore be subject to laws applicable in the jurisdictions where those providers operate.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for transfers of personal information.

14. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning information associated with you.

These rights may include the ability to:

  • Request access to information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion
  • Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent
  • Object to certain processing
  • Restrict certain processing
  • Request information about how data is handled
  • Request a portable copy of certain information where applicable
  • Opt out of certain communications

Many Scout preferences may be changed directly through your account.

For requests that cannot be completed through Scout's account controls, contact Scout through its official Contact or Support page.

Scout may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

15. Communications

Scout may send communications relating to:

  • Account verification
  • Password recovery
  • Account security
  • Service activity
  • Job alerts
  • Saved searches
  • Application reminders
  • Subscriptions
  • Billing
  • Important Service changes

Optional communications may include an unsubscribe or preference-management mechanism where required.

Some essential account, security, transactional, or legal communications may still be sent even if you disable optional messages.

16. Children

Scout is not intended for users who are below the minimum age permitted under applicable law.

Scout does not knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so would violate applicable law.

If Scout learns that information was collected in circumstances where it should not have been collected, reasonable steps will be taken to delete it.

Users who have not reached the age of majority should use Scout with the involvement of a parent or guardian where required.

17. Third-Party Services and Links

Scout may contain links to:

  • Employers
  • Applicant tracking systems
  • Job boards
  • Recruiting services
  • Other third-party websites or services

Scout is not responsible for the privacy, security, accuracy, availability, or practices of independent third parties.

Review their terms and privacy policies before providing them with information.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Scout may update this Privacy Policy as the Service changes.

When changes are made, the effective date at the top of this policy will be updated.

If a change materially affects how personal information is handled, Scout may provide additional notice through the Service or another appropriate method.

Where applicable law requires consent to a change, Scout will obtain the required consent.

19. Contact

Questions, concerns, or requests relating to privacy may be submitted through Scout's official Contact or Support page.

Privacy-related requests should be clearly identified so they can be handled appropriately.
