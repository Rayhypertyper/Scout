Install the browser helper

Load the unpacked extension from the Scout project on this computer.

  1. In Chromium, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Choose Load unpacked, then select the extension/ folder inside your Scout project checkout.
  3. Sign in to Scout in this browser profile. Open the helper, choose Set up, enter this Scout address, then connect and approve access to that address to sync your profile and saved answers.

Scout address your current Scout address

The helper is installed from this local project folder. Scout does not report an installed or connected state on this page.

Use it on an application

  1. Open the role’s application page on its ATS site.
  2. Choose a local PDF or DOCX resume or cover letter in the helper if you want to attach one.
  3. Select Fill blank fields, then review every filled field and attachment and complete anything it missed.
  4. Submit the application yourself. With company and role filled in, the helper can watch a supported page for up to 30 minutes after a trusted submit event. A new confirmation may be logged to your connected Scout account; otherwise, choose “I submitted this application” after you submit.

Automatic tracking requires a connected, verified Scout account. Review company and role before filling. If Scout is unavailable, a confirmed log waits locally until Scout is open. Re-read exact work authorization and sponsorship answers against the application's wording.

You stay in control

Profile data syncs only after you connect the helper. The local helper checks the active form when you choose Check page or Fill blank fields. Autofill inserts matching values into the employer’s page under that site’s data practices; you review the form and decide whether to submit it.
