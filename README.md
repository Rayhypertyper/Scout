# Best Weather Finder ☀️

A simple app that finds the **best weather in your city** by comparing conditions across different areas.

## Features

* Compare weather across the city
* Find the sunniest and warmest areas
* Avoid rain and poor conditions
* Get the best location automatically

## How It Works

Enter your city and the app compares local weather data to determine which area currently has the best conditions.
